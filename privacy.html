Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Welcome to the London Ravens Volleyball Club. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in the principle of data minimisation. Our website does not use active registration forms, account logins, or background analytics trackers to directly harvest your personal data. You can browse our site anonymously.

2. Third-Party Services and Cookies

We have intentionally designed this website to minimize third-party tracking and respect your privacy:

  • Location Maps: We use a static image API to display our venue locations. Unlike interactive maps, this static image does not place tracking cookies on your device, ensuring your browsing remains private.
  • YouTube: We use YouTube's "privacy-enhanced mode" (youtube-nocookie.com) to embed our team highlight videos. This ensures that YouTube does not store information about you on your device unless you actively click to play the video.

3. Contacting Us

If you reach out to us using the provided email links (e.g., info@londonravens.com), your default email application will open. We will receive your email address and any personal details you choose to share in your message. We use this information solely to answer your questions or provide information about joining the club. We do not sell your data or add you to marketing mailing lists without your explicit, prior consent.

4. Data Security and Retention

We treat all communications with strict confidentiality. Your emails and inquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to resolve your query or facilitate your participation in our tryouts and training sessions, after which they are securely deleted.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you hold several rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten").

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in web standards, our club practices, or legal obligations. Any updates will be published directly on this page.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how the London Ravens manage data, please contact us at info@londonravens.com.
