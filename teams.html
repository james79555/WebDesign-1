Women's 1st Team

Competing in the top division of the London Regional League, our flagship squad is built for experienced athletes seeking high-intensity drills and advanced tactical coaching. Perfect for former university players chasing top league honors.

Requirement: Two practice sessions per week plus match days.

Mens 1st Team

Playing at the highest regional level, this squad runs fast-paced, complex offenses. We expect top-tier technical proficiency, physical fitness, and a fierce drive to dominate the court.

Requirement: Two practice sessions per week plus match days.

Mens 2nd Team (Development Squad)

Bridging the gap between social play and fierce competition. Ideal for intermediate players wanting to refine fundamentals, learn advanced rotational systems, and compete locally in a highly supportive environment.

Requirement: One practice session per week plus match days.

Tryouts and Open Sessions

Roster spots are highly competitive. Official open trials are held every August before the September season launch, where our coaching staff evaluates technical ability, tactical awareness, and overall team fit.

Moving to London mid-season? If you have high-level regional or university experience, reach out via our contact links below. We occasionally invite exceptional prospective players to guest-train with the squads.
