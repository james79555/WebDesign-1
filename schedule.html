All of our home games are played at our primary fortress: Roehampton Sport & Fitness Centre. If you are a prospective player looking to watch a game to get a feel for our club's culture, just look for the players in the black and green warm-up gear and come say hello!

The Match Day Experience

Experience the electric atmosphere at Laverstoke Gardens! Entry is always free. Pack the sidelines, bring the noise, and help us defend our home court. Stick around afterwards for MVP announcements and post-game socials.

Training Sessions

Join us every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday (7:00 PM - 9:00 PM) at Roehampton Sport & Fitness Centre. All skill levels are welcome - whether you are returning to the sport or just looking to play socially, we have a spot for you.

What to Bring To Training

Arrive 15 minutes early to assist with the nets and warm up. Bring plenty of water, knee pads, and indoor court shoes (non-marking soles only). We provide the match balls and all training equipment.
