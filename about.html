More Than Just a Club. We are the Ravens!

Welcome to the London Ravens! Based in the heart of London, we balance fierce competition with a thriving, social community. We believe in working hard on the court and celebrating together off it.

Whether you are a seasoned graduate seeking high-level play, or an intermediate player looking for fitness and lifelong friends, you'll find a home here. With expert coaching and weekly socials, the Ravens are always expanding our flock.

Our Coaching Philosophy

We don't just run drills; we build volleyball IQ. Drawing on National Volleyball League (NVL) and international experience, our coaches deliver dynamic sessions focused on biomechanics, advanced rotational systems, and mental resilience. Train with us to become a smarter, more efficient athlete.

Commitment to Grassroots Growth

While our 1st teams fight for promotion, our heart remains in grassroots development. We provide a highly inclusive environment for intermediate players to bridge the gap to competitive play, proudly driving the growth of volleyball in South West London.
